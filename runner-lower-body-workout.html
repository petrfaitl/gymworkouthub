🏃 Runner’s Lower‑Body Strength

10 exercises · build resilience · stay healthy

📏 185 cm | ⚖️ 77 kg | 🏃 Runner
Reps: 6–8 per side
Sets: 4 rounds
Tempo: 3s ecc · 1s con
Rest: 60–90s rest
⚡ Weight selection: Choose a load that allows perfect form for 6–8 reps. The last 2 should be challenging but stable.
bilateral · dumbbells

① Reverse Lunge (off step)

How: Stand on a step, dumbbells in hand. Step back, front foot planted. Drive through front foot.
Targets: quads · glutes · balance

🏋️ 10–14 kg each hand
▶ Watch demo
unilateral · single dumbbell

② Lateral Side Squat

How: Wide stance, goblet hold. Shift weight to one side, keep other leg straight.
Targets: adductors · glute medius

🏋️ 16–20 kg single
▶ Watch demo
plyometric · bodyweight

③ Box Drop (rebound & hold)

How: Step off box, land softly on one leg. Rebound immediately if reactive power is the focus.
Targets: landing control · power

🏋️ Bodyweight (+4-8kg adv)
▶ Watch demo
unilateral · single dumbbell

④ Bulgarian Split Squat

How: Rear foot on bench, front foot forward. Hold one DB. Lower until thigh is parallel.
Targets: quads · stability

🏋️ 12–16 kg single
▶ Watch demo
bilateral · dumbbells

⑤ Squat (heel wedge)

How: Heels on wedge, DBs on shoulders. Squat deep keeping core tight.
Targets: quads · ankle flexion

🏋️ 10–14 kg each hand
▶ Watch demo
unilateral · dumbbell

⑥ Weighted Calf Raise

How: Wall support for balance. One DB in same-side hand. Rise slowly, squeeze, lower with control.
Targets: gastrocnemius · stability

🏋️ 12–16 kg single
▶ Watch demo
unilateral · dumbbell

⑦ Soleus Calf Raise

How: Lean against wall, support knee bent to 90°. Move ONLY the ankle. fixed knee angle.
Targets: deep calf · push‑off

🏋️ 10–14 kg single
▶ Watch demo
unilateral · dumbbell

⑧ Hip Airplane (rotation)

How: Hinge forward on one leg. Rotate pelvis open toward stance leg, then close.
Targets: glute med · stability

🏋️ 6–10 kg single
▶ Watch demo
bilateral · dumbbells

⑨ Romanian Deadlift (RDL)

How: Hinge at hips, slight knee bend. Lower DBs along shins until hamstring stretch.
Targets: posterior chain · glutes

🏋️ 14–20 kg each hand
▶ Watch demo
unilateral · two dumbbells

⑩ Deep Bulgarian Split Squat

How: Rear foot on bench, hold two DBs. Lower into deep range for maximum activation.
Targets: quads · deep strength

🏋️ 10–14 kg each hand
▶ Watch demo
🔁 6–8 reps / side · 4 sets
⏱️ ~45–55 min · 2–3x / week
📌 Running‑specific notes:
  • Perform unilateral movements weak side first, match reps.
  • For landing exercises, focus on soft, quiet landings.
  • All calf raises should be done with slow, controlled tempo.
  • Best performed after a run or on non‑running days.
