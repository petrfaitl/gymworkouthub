📋 Training Notes & Nutrition

🎯 Philosophy

  • Build muscle safely, not test max strength.
  • Master form first. No technique sacrifice.
  • Leave 2 reps in reserve.
  • Slow eccentrics (2–3s down).

📈 Progression

  • Increase weight by 2.5 kg when form is perfect.
  • Consistency beats intensity.
  • 2 sessions/week is plenty.

🥣 Nutrition Advice

🔥 Calorie target: 2,700–3,000 / day

🥩 Protein: 90–110 g / day

• High-calorie smoothies are key

• Hydrate consistently

💡 Bulk Tip

"If not gaining ~0.5kg/week after 2-3 weeks, add another snack. Swimming burns a lot of fuel."

🛌 Recovery & Safety
  • Sleep 8–10 hours per night.
  • Always keep shoulder blades "down and back".
  • Report any joint pain immediately.
