🏃 Runner’s Lower‑Body Strength & Injury Prevention 10 exercises · build resilience · stay healthy

📏 185 cm  |  ⚖️ 77 kg  |  🏃 runner
Reps:6–8 per side
Sets:4
Tempo:eccentric 3s · concentric 1s · pause 1s
Rest:60–90 s between sets
⚡ Weight selection: Choose a load that allows perfect form for 6–8 reps. The last 2 reps should be challenging but without breaking technique. Suggested ranges below are starting points for a 77 kg runner – adjust up or down based on your strength.
① Reverse Lunge (off step)
bilateral · dumbbells
How: Stand on a small step, holding a dumbbell in each hand. Step one foot back onto the step, front foot planted. Lower until both knees are ~90°, front knee tracking over toes. Drive through the front foot to return. Alternate sides.
Target: quads · glutes · balance
🏋️ Suggested weight: 10–14 kg each hand

② Lateral Side Squat (goblet hold)
unilateral · single dumbbell
How: Stand with feet wider than shoulders, toes slightly turned out. Hold one dumbbell vertically against your chest (goblet style). Shift weight to one side, bend that knee while the other leg stays straight. Keep your chest up; push back to center.
Target: adductors · glute medius · hip mobility
🏋️ Suggested weight: 16–20 kg single
Watch demo
③ Box Drop (rebound & hold)
plyometric · bodyweight
How: Stand on a box (20–40 cm). Step off (not jump) and land softly on one leg, holding the landing position for 2–3 seconds. For a rebound, immediately jump back up or perform a countermovement jump.
Target: eccentric strength · landing control · reactive power
🏋️ Suggested weight: bodyweight (add 4–8 kg for advanced)
Watch demo
④ Bulgarian Split Squat (single DB)
unilateral · single dumbbell
How: Place your rear foot on a bench/box, front foot forward. Hold one dumbbell by your side or at your chest. Lower until your front thigh is parallel and rear knee almost touches the floor. Drive up through the front heel.
Target: quads · glutes · single‑leg stability
🏋️ Suggested weight: 12–16 kg single
Watch demo
⑤ Squat (shoulder‑held DBs, heel wedge)
bilateral · dumbbells
How: Stand with heels on a small wedge or plate, feet shoulder‑width. Hold a dumbbell in each hand, resting them on your shoulders (or overhead). Keeping your core tight, squat down until thighs are at least parallel. Drive up.
Target: quads (emphasis) · core · ankle dorsiflexion
🏋️ Suggested weight: 10–14 kg each hand
Watch demo
⑥ Weighted Calf Raise (wall‑supported)
unilateral · dumbbell
How: Stand near a wall for balance. Raise one leg (knee bent) off the floor. Hold a dumbbell in the same‑side hand. Place the ball of your foot on a raised surface; slowly rise onto your toes, squeeze your calf, then lower with control.
Target: gastrocnemius · ankle stability
🏋️ Suggested weight: 12–16 kg single
Watch demo
⑦ Soleus Calf Raise (bent knee)
unilateral · dumbbell
How: Lean against a wall with your support knee bent to ~90° (mimicking a seated position). Hold a dumbbell on the same side. Place the ball of your foot on a block; perform the raise by only moving your ankle – keep the knee angle fixed.
Target: soleus (deep calf) · push‑off power
🏋️ Suggested weight: 10–14 kg single
Watch demo
⑧ Hip Airplane (rotation)
unilateral · dumbbell
How: Stand on one leg, holding a wall for balance. Tilt your torso forward while the other leg extends straight back. Rotate your pelvis open toward the stance leg (like an airplane wing). Hold a dumbbell in the opposite hand for load.
Target: glute medius · deep hip rotators · pelvic stability
🏋️ Suggested weight: 6–10 kg single (balance first)
Watch demo
⑨ Romanian Deadlift (RDL)
bilateral · dumbbells
How: Stand with feet hip‑width apart, holding a dumbbell in each hand. With a slight bend in your knees, push your hips back and hinge forward, lowering the dumbbells along your shins until you feel a stretch in your hamstrings. Drive your hips forward to stand.
Target: hamstrings · glutes · posterior chain
🏋️ Suggested weight: 14–20 kg each hand
Watch demo
⑩ Deep Bulgarian Split Squat (2 DBs)
unilateral · two dumbbells
How: Rear foot on a bench, hold a dumbbell in each hand by your sides. Lower into a deeper range – rear knee closer to the floor and front knee travelling further forward. Keep your torso upright; push through the front foot to return.
Target: quads (deep range) · glutes · unilateral strength
🏋️ Suggested weight: 10–14 kg each hand
Watch demo
🔁 All exercises 6–8 reps / side · 4 sets
⏱️ Total time: ~45–55 min  ·  2–3 times / week

📌 Running‑specific notes:

  • Perform unilateral movements weak side first, and match reps to that side.
  • For landing exercises (box drop), focus on soft, quiet landings – knees track over toes, no valgus.
  • All calf raises should be done with slow, controlled tempo (3s down, 1s up) to maximise stretch.
  • Best performed after a run or on non‑running days.
