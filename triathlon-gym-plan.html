📝 Explanations & Strategy

🎯 Why these exercises?

  • Swim: Lat pulldown and overhead press build pulling strength.
  • Bike: Hip thrusts and RDLs develop power for aero position.
  • Run: Single‑leg work improves economy and balance.
  • Overall: Core work keeps you stable when fatigued.

🗓️ Combining with S/B/R

  • Tue/Thu (Gym + Bike): Gym first, then easy 20-30 min spin (Zone 2).
  • Mon/Fri (Swim only): Low-impact recovery days.
  • Weekend (Long S/B/R): Keep Thu as last gym day (48h buffer).

💡 Final tip

"This gym work is supplemental. Your race performance comes from S/B/R volume. Use this plan to build a robust foundation."
