📌 Ongoing Load Management
  • No more than ~10% week‑to‑week running volume increase.
  • Monitor training load spikes around key blocks/races.
  • Review cadence if overstriding present (target 5–10% increase if indicated).
  • Any return of shin pain → pause plyometric progression and consult your physio.

Objective markers to track: L/R symmetry <10% in hip/knee strength, single‑leg heel raise capacity (target 25 reps, LSI <10%), and landing force symmetry in CMJ.
