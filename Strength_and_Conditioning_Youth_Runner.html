🏋️ Strength & Conditioning for a young middle‑distance runner

🧑‍🦰 Female teen · 51 kg · 165 cm · little prior strength work
Frequency: Friday gym session
Duration: ~45 min
Goal: Build resilience, strength & power
⚠️ Injury history: History of tibial stress injuries.
✅ Only progress to the next circuit if the previous block was fully pain‑free.
✅ Stop any exercise that produces sharp arch or shin pain – mild muscle fatigue is normal, sharp pain is not.

Daily / Frequent Shin‑Calf Maintenance — 4–5× / week

🧠 Why: These low‑impact exercises are the foundation of injury prevention. Aim to do them after most runs and always during the Friday gym session.

📌 Progression rules & weight guidelines
  • Start light – the suggested weights are conservative starting points for a 51 kg teen with little strength training. Increase by 1–2 kg per week only when you can complete all reps with perfect form.
  • Daily maintenance (heel drops, toe raises, calf raises) should be done 4–5× per week, not just on Fridays.
  • Cutback weeks (4, 8, 12, 16, 17) – reduce sets by ~30% or do only mobility/activation; this is deliberate.
  • Never progress to the next circuit if you experienced any shin pain in the previous block. Repeat the current circuit and consult your physio.
  • Track and hill sessions are always followed by an easy day – preserve that buffer to protect the tibia.
⏱️ Each circuit ~45 min · 2–3× per week (Friday plus optional extra day)
