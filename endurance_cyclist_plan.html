Goal: Maintain bike‑specific strength when outdoor rides aren’t possible
Frequency: 2–3× / week (substitute for missed rides)
Duration: 45–60 min

Session C — Bad‑Weather Alternative missed 4‑hr ride substitute

🌧️ Use this when: weather or schedule forces you to skip a long outdoor ride, and you can’t get on the turbo trainer.
Goal: Combine muscular endurance + aerobic stimulus to keep your 160 km prep on track.

⏱️ Session outline:

  • Warm‑up: 10 min stationary bike (easy spin, Z1, < 140 W)
  • Main circuit: 4–5 rounds of the exercises below (all weights listed here), with 60 s rest between rounds
  • Finish: 15–20 min stationary bike @ Z3 tempo (195–215 W), RPE 6–7/10
Circuit exercises (repeat 4–5×)
Goblet Squat

12 reps
Form: Dumbbell against chest, squat deep, drive up.
⚖️ 12–16 kg (single dumbbell)
Watch demo
Dumbbell RDL

Form: Hinge at hips, lower dumbbells along shins, feel the stretch, drive up.
⚖️ 2 × 12–16 kg (dumbbells)
Watch demo

⚖️ 2 × 8–10 kg (dumbbells by sides)

🚴 Finish on the bike: After the circuit, do 15–20 min at Z3 tempo (195–215 W) on a stationary bike. This ensures you get the aerobic stimulus you’d have had from the missed outdoor ride. Keep cadence 85–95 rpm.
📈 Progression & load guidelines (76 kg athlete):
  • Start at the lower end of each weight range. Add 2–5 kg per week only when you complete all reps with perfect form.
  • RPE 7–8 (out of 10) on the last 2 reps of each set is the sweet spot – challenging but not maxing out.
  • On weeks with 3 outdoor rides, do only 1–2 gym sessions (prioritise recovery).
  • On poor‑weather weeks, you can replace one missed ride with Session C, or do Session A + 30 min of hard intervals on the stationary bike.
  • Taper: Reduce gym volume by ~30% in the final 7–10 days before your 160 km event – fresh legs win on race day.
⏱️ Sessions A & B: ~45–60 min · Session C: ~50 min (including bike warm‑up & finish)
