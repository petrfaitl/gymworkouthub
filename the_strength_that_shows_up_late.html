THE STRENGTH THAT SHOWS UP LATE

A 50-minute gym session for runners

50:00

The Pay-off

The pay-off rarely shows up on a fresh time trial. It shows up as how much a runner slows over the last third of a race. Heavy lifting plus plyometrics, twice a week, six to fourteen weeks before judging it.

Programming Snapshot

  • Timing: 3+ hrs after run, 24+ hrs before key session
  • Frequency: 2x a week (lifts + plyo same session)
  • Time to Effect: 6-14 weeks minimum
01

Warm-up & Activation

~10 minutes

Single Leg

One leg, all the load, no help from the other side.

Exercises

Step-up Split squat Single-leg RDL

Start with Step-up. Build to Single-leg RDL.

Coach's Eye: full foot down, knee tracks well, pelvis stays level.

Recommended Load

Bodyweight initially. RDLs: 5-8kg dumbbells, progressing to 12-15kg.
Bodyweight or light dumbbells (10-15kg). RDLs: 15-20kg dumbbells, progressing to 25-30kg.
Evidence & Programming ▼

Evidence: Single-leg RDLs in the warm-up cut hamstring-strain risk by two-thirds across a season (Otani et al. 2025).

Sets/Reps: 2-4 sets x 8-12 reps (base) or 3-8 reps (trained). Rest: 60-180s.

"Put single-leg work first in the program, not last."

Power & Plyometric Support

This is where strength turns into speed.

Exercises

Pogo hops Squat jump Bounds

Start with Pogo hops. Build to Bounds.

Coach's Eye: fast contacts, quality first, stop before intent fades.

Recommended Load

Bodyweight only. Focus on landing mechanics and soft, quiet contacts.
Bodyweight, or very light dumbbells (5kg) for squat jumps if joints allow. Emphasize quality over height.
Evidence & Programming ▼

Evidence: Combined heavy plus plyometric training gave the largest effect on running performance of any method reviewed (Llanos-Lagos et al. 2024).

Sets/Reps: 3-6 sets x 2-5 reps. Rest: 60-180s.

"Pair the jump with the lift. Don’t swap one for the other."

02

Main Strength

~20 minutes

Anterior Chain

Squat heavy and your push-off gets stronger.

Exercises

Goblet squat Front squat Back squat

Start with Goblet squat. Build to Back squat.

Coach's Eye: own the range, brace well, stop before form breaks.

Recommended Load

Goblet: 10-16kg. Squat (form solid): 20-30kg base, progressing to 35-45kg heavy sets.
Goblet: 20-30kg. Squat: 40-60kg base, progressing to 70-90kg+ heavy sets.
Evidence & Programming ▼

Evidence: Heavy squats beat plyometrics alone for running economy and time-trial performance (Eihara et al. 2022).

Sets/Reps: 2-4 sets x 8-12 reps (base) or 3-8 reps at 80%+ 1RM (trained). Rest: 60-180s.

"Heavy squats move running economy further than jumping ever will alone."

Posterior Chain

Your hips drive more of the propulsion than most runners give them credit for.

Exercises

Glute bridge Romanian deadlift Trap bar deadlift

Start with Glute bridge. Build to Trap bar deadlift.

Coach's Eye: long spine, clear hip hinge, feel it in the glutes and hamstrings.

Recommended Load

Glute bridge: Bodyweight. RDL: 20-30kg base, 35-50kg heavy. Trap bar: 30-40kg base, 50-60kg heavy.
Glute bridge: Bodyweight or barbell (40-60kg). RDL: 40-60kg base, 70-100kg+ heavy. Trap bar: 60-80kg base, 100-130kg+ heavy.
Evidence & Programming ▼

Evidence: Heavy-load training (≥80% 1RM) lifts running economy, more at higher speeds (Llanos-Lagos et al. 2024).

Sets/Reps: 2-4 sets x 8-12 reps (base) or 3-8 reps at 80%+ 1RM (trained). Rest: 60-180s.

"Load the hinge as hard as the squat."

03

Accessory & Stability

~15 minutes

Trunk & Anti-Rotation

Dead bug Side plank Farmer carry Pallof press

Coach's Eye: ribs stacked over pelvis, resist the wobble.

Recommended Load

Farmer carry: 10-15kg/hand. Pallof: 5-10kg band.
Farmer carry: 20-30kg/hand. Pallof: 15-25kg cable.

Frontal Plane & Hip

Single-leg bridge Band walk Copenhagen plank

Coach's Eye: stay tall, no collapse through the hip.

Recommended Load

Bridge: BW. Band walk: Light/Med band. Copenhagen: Short lever.
Bridge: BW/5-10kg plate. Band walk: Med/Heavy band. Copenhagen: Long lever.

Calf & Foot

Standing calf raise Bent-knee calf raise Tibialis raise

Coach's Eye: full range, slow lower, pause at the top.

Recommended Load

Calf raises: BW to 10-20kg. Tibialis: BW/light band.
Calf raises: BW to 20-40kg. Tibialis: BW/band.
